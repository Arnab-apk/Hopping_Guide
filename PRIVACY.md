# Privacy Policy — Kolkata Puja

> Host this file via GitHub Pages so you have a public URL for the Play Store
> Data Safety form and privacy policy requirement (architecture doc section 6).

## What we collect

- **Account info:** your Google account name, email, and profile photo, used
  only to identify you within a group. This comes from Google Sign-In and is
  managed by Firebase Authentication.
- **Location data:** when you actively choose to share your location within a
  group, your device's latitude and longitude are sent to our backend
  (Firebase Realtime Database) and shown to the other members of that group.
  Location sharing is **foreground-only** — it works while you are actively
  using the app, and stops when you leave or close the app. We do **not**
  collect background location.

## How we use it

- To show pandal markers and sort the "nearest pandals to me" list.
- To display your position to group members you have chosen to share with.

## What we do NOT do

- We do not sell your data.
- We do not use your data for cross-app advertising.
- We do not access your location unless you explicitly enable sharing.

## Data retention

- Live location is ephemeral and removed when you stop sharing / disconnect.
- You can leave a group at any time to stop sharing with its members.

## Third parties

- Firebase (Google) for authentication, database, and messaging.
- OpenStreetMap contributors for map tiles.
- OpenRouteService for optional walking directions (P1).

## Contact

Open an issue on the project repository for privacy questions.
